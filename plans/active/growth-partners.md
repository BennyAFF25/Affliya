2. Growth role is creator_partners.program + user_id; profiles role remains business/affiliate. Approval links existing auth identities or creates role-less identities. Auth magic-link confirm uses auth-helpers. Explicit marketplace opt-in creates affiliate profile only when absent.

4. Replace commission basis with collected monthly subscription revenue excluding tax, discounts, credits and other fees, capped by collection. AUD without reported tax removes 1/11 by default. Three successful pending/approved/paid periods per business across subscription IDs; fully reversed unpaid periods release a slot, while refunded paid periods remain counted. Suspended partners excluded; default 14-day approval hold. Unique invoice and audit keys; refunds reduce unpaid amounts or create negative clawbacks after payment. Transactional SQL needed wherever concurrent financial mutations require serialization.
5. Hidden noindex application/login/dashboard and allowlisted internal admin. Canonical renderer, guarded nonproduction recipients, best-effort Lead reporting with shared event ID, no secrets/tokens logged.
