# Refund policy, terms acceptance and billing emails

**Status:** in progress — built on staging behind flags (27 Nov 2026). Production needs Ben's own approval, the lawyer-reviewed Terms text and production migrations.

**Problem:** Growth trial customers had no recorded acceptance (browsewrap), no refund policy, a dynamic "Last updated" date, a day-12 reminder only for the newest signup cohort, and no trial-started / receipt / cancellation / refund emails. Failed-payment emails existed but their table was missing in production. Evidence and specs: `/workspace/nettmark/legal/refund-policy-2026-11-27/` (01–05), Grok Affiliate brief approved by Ben for a staging build.

**Product intent:** explicit founder instruction (relayed brief, staging approval). Keeps the card-required 14-day trial and A$49/month price unchanged; makes the terms, auto-renewal and cancellation path explicit before the charge (Visa trial rules, ACL, UCT). Aligns with "hide infrastructure, expose outcomes": one summary box and one checkbox, no extra step.

**Implementation (flags default off):**
- Part A — `utils/legal/version.ts` (TERMS_VERSION `terms-2026-12-v2`, fixed publish date), `app/components/legal/TermsOfServiceV2Content.tsx` (clause 7, ACL carve-outs on 12/13, new clause 14 variation text, entity/ABN, SA governing law), fixed dates on all three policies, IP/UA line in Privacy, refund summary on /pricing and the for-businesses FAQ. Flag `NEXT_PUBLIC_TERMS_V2_ENABLED`; the legacy wording is untouched while off.
- Part B — `legal_acceptances` + `profiles.terms_version` migration; `POST /api/legal/accept`; signup checkbox enforced in `/api/auth/signup` (records `signup` with IP/UA); trial/paid summary + checkbox in the trial-first continuation, the choose-plan Growth card and the activation modal; `create-checkout-session` returns 409 `terms_required` without a fresh (30 min) `trial_start`/`paid_start` acceptance and adds `terms_version`/`terms_acceptance_id` to metadata and the idempotency key; Stripe `consent_collection` + `custom_text` behind `CHECKOUT_TOS_CONSENT_ENABLED`. AcceptTermsModal posts to the route and reappears when the stored version differs.
- Part C — `business_billing_notifications` ledger; webhook enqueue (E1/E4/E6/E7/E8); 5-minute `billing-notifications` cron; trial reminders E3 for all cohorts based on trial end (`TRIAL_BILLING_REMINDERS_V2_ENABLED`) and optional E2 (`NETTMARK_DAY7_TRIAL_REMINDER`); E5 copy in `utils/billingRecovery.ts`.

**Trade-offs / risks:** Terms text is a draft; publish date placeholder 1 Dec 2026. Vercel crons don't run on Preview, so QA calls the cron routes manually with CRON_SECRET. The new 5-minute cron only takes effect on a production deploy. Refund detection maps charge → invoice via `invoicePayments` (API basil has no `charge.invoice`); the Growth Partner refund reversal still reads `charge.invoice` (pre-existing, flagged). Google/OAuth signups (if any) are covered by the modal, not the signup checkbox.
