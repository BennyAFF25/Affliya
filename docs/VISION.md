# Nettmark Vision

## Why Nettmark exists

Nettmark exists to make distribution accessible to ordinary people.

Paid advertising has shown that the right distribution can turn small businesses into major brands, but access to that opportunity is still concentrated among business owners, agencies, media buyers, experienced marketers, and people who already have large audiences.

Nettmark asks a simple question:

Why can’t an ordinary person put money behind the distribution of a business they believe in and earn a commission when that distribution generates a sale?

Traditional affiliate platforms often favour people who already have an audience, experience, or an existing distribution channel. Nettmark is designed to remove that barrier and give ordinary people the infrastructure to participate.

The long-term ambition is for becoming an ambassador for a business to feel almost effortless — potentially as simple as scanning a Nettmark QR code on a product — and eventually allowing people to allocate money toward distributing businesses and products they believe in.

## Who Nettmark is for

Nettmark is built for both businesses and affiliates.

A first-time affiliate should open Nettmark and immediately feel like they have a genuine opportunity to compete, even without an existing audience, sophisticated infrastructure, or professional marketing experience.

A business should open Nettmark and quickly understand the value of accessing a network of people willing to create content, promote products, and potentially fund advertising on its behalf.

The platform should aim to create a practical win-win relationship between both sides.

## The end state

At scale, Nettmark should become a distribution network.

The platform should increasingly work for the user rather than require the user to operate the platform manually.

For affiliates, that means progressively automating or assisting areas such as:

- creative generation
- campaign setup
- optimisation
- recommendations
- approvals
- budgeting assistance
- identifying the next best action

For businesses, it means making access to distributed promotion, content and advertising increasingly simple and predictable.

The long-term experience should feel closer to participating in an ecosystem than operating advertising software.

Nettmark should make contributing to the growth of an existing brand feel as accessible as other mainstream ways people allocate money toward opportunities.

## The network effect

Nettmark should become stronger as both sides grow.

Businesses should have incentives to bring affiliates into the ecosystem.

Affiliates should have incentives to bring businesses into the ecosystem.

The goal is a network where distribution compounds through participation rather than relying entirely on Nettmark itself to recruit both sides.

## Core promise to businesses

Businesses gain access to measurable distribution with controlled and predictable economics.

The distinguishing feature is that affiliates can do more than share links or promote organically — they can fund and operate paid advertising where the business allows it.

This means businesses can potentially gain advertising exposure without directly funding the media spend themselves, while primarily paying commissions when measurable outcomes occur.

Businesses remain free to run their own advertising alongside Nettmark.

## Core promise to affiliates

Affiliates gain access to businesses, offers and infrastructure that would otherwise require significant knowledge, systems or existing influence to access.

Nettmark should allow someone ordinary to participate in the distribution of businesses that are already operating and potentially growing.

The platform should give affiliates the infrastructure needed to move from simply sharing affiliate links toward genuinely participating in distribution.

## What Nettmark should never become

Nettmark should not become dependent on business models where attribution is inherently difficult or unreliable.

The platform should prioritise businesses where sales, conversions and commercial outcomes can be measured with reasonable confidence.

It should avoid becoming a platform associated with:

- get-rich-quick schemes
- course-selling ecosystems built around Nettmark
- misleading promises of easy income
- businesses that cannot provide reliable attribution
- questionable or illegal operations
- third-party programmes that do not represent genuine brands or direct business relationships

## Strategic direction

Paid advertising is Nettmark's primary differentiator and remains strategically important to the product message.

However, organic promotion should become at least as strong as competing affiliate and creator platforms.

The objective is to compete strongly in traditional affiliate functionality while retaining the paid-distribution capability that gives Nettmark its own category.

Nettmark should therefore grow as a combination of:

- distribution network
- affiliate marketplace
- creator ecosystem
- advertising infrastructure
- increasingly intelligent operating layer

The unifying idea is distribution.

## What success looks like

Nettmark is working when:

- affiliates are confidently funding paid advertising
- more businesses are connecting the systems required to participate fully
- genuine attributed sales are occurring
- paid trials are converting into paying customers
- businesses and affiliates remain engaged after onboarding
- the platform is becoming easier to use as its capabilities grow

## The product destination

The implementation of Nettmark will continuously change.

Workflows, architecture, UI, internal systems and individual features should be treated as mouldable.

The destination is not.

The product should continually move toward making distribution easier, more accessible and more useful for ordinary people and businesses.

Every Nettmark product decision should ultimately make it easier for affiliates and businesses to connect and grow without relying on traditional networking, agencies or existing distribution infrastructure.
